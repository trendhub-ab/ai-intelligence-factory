## Non-negotiable editorial objective

P5 Writer is an intellectually curious 17-year-old, but **does not perform age or gender**. Natural neutral Japanese, theme-specific naïve questions, surprise grounded in Evidence, a visible change of understanding, and reader discovery are the goal. Do not substitute fake memories, exclamations, repeated rhetorical questions, `私は驚いた`, generic advice, or corporate/consultant summaries.

The original five-stage deliverables are **Writer Reaction → Editorial Discovery → Cognitive Shift → Persona Article Generation → Fact-Safe Review**. Article Generation must carry forward the Writer's actual concrete question and reveal *why the evidence changed the view*. Fact-Safe Review may repair only fact/claim inaccuracies, never sand away a supported authorial discovery as mere style. Structural completeness alone does not prove either behavior.

## Promotion gates: two orthogonal layers

1. **Technical (provider-free, automatable)**: selected prompts equal across three golden cases; original baseline inputs pinned; pure call config without extra persona; no Preview/Lite; stage JSON complete; no persistence path; publication unchanged. CI pass means *technical readiness only*.
2. **Editorial (HUMAN blind evaluation)**: identical per-case source/qualifiers and the archived 83-point reference draft; **different Gemini models across samples/cases are allowed by explicit owner decision on 2026-10-24**. Label the model for every candidate and score article quality directly: opening hook, writer-owned question, evidence-based discovery, thought/feeling movement, naturalness, subject-specific uniqueness, claim accuracy. A mixed-model editorial score can establish whether a manuscript is fun to read, **not** whether an observed delta was caused by a prompt, model or integration change. If a *causal ablation* is desired, only then require same model/config/input for the arms. The 83-point historical aggregate is a reference **not** a valid controlled 83→68 delta. Require fresh human editorial signoff and target score ≥90 before any Production rollout; NO-GO otherwise.

**Explicitly prohibited:** promote based on pytest count, question mark quota, invented emotion, one successful sample, **causal claims from mixed-model arms**, or post-hoc editorial wording adjustments without human scoring. Do not merge PR #861/#862, alter default Classic, relax Fact/Evidence/Publication Gate, or generate/publish notes without owner authorization.

Never assert that matching a prompt alone restores 83-point reader quality.

## Implementation patch: fail-closed P0 paths (October 24)

1. **No P5-to-Classic writer bypass:** `a_plus_editorial_orchestration.py` suppresses `force_local_fallback` and the ordinary quality retry for `AIIF_EDITORIAL_STYLE=v3_p5`. A direct `quality_retry` provider call also fails before contacting Gemini. The existing retry/Local Skills behavior for `classic` is unchanged. A P5 article that cannot pass its own authorial requirements remains unapproved.
2. **Actual original P5 on NEW input, nonpublishing only:** With `AIIF_P5_WRITER_BOUNDARY=original`, the original Writer becomes eligible for `request_origin=new` only while `generate_intelligence_report(..., persist_results=False)` is active. The wrapper never trusts an environment variable alone as proof of nonpersistence. Any `v3_p5` request with `persist_results=True` is refused for both boundaries until a genuine human approval authority is implemented. Normal Daily/default Classic is unchanged.
3. **No additional creative system persona:** Original P5 writer uses the selected five-stage source prompt without the added Production creative persona/management skeleton. The first management call and independent Evidence/Fact/Publication checks stay in their existing pipeline, but this *does not* prove identical historical material or a score of 83.
4. **P5 provider and quota boundary:** `local_skills/v3_p5_rate_contract.py` orders only `gemini-3.8-flash → gemini-3.6-flash`. For P5 Deep Dive, a send guard installed at the native `_generate_via_chat` entry counts both management and Writer calls, including pool retries, and refuses a fourth Gemini 3.6 logical SDK call before requesting it. The P5 send temporarily lowers the existing repository-persistent Gemini 3.6 per-model daily cap to **3**, so earlier calls by other runs in this repository consume the same daily allowance; after each request the ordinary Classic cap is restored. If the repository persistent counter is disabled, 3.6 P5 sends are rejected. For any nonpublishing P5 validation (managed or original), the first management request also uses the ordered canonical provider pool directly; this prevents Run260 provider-health ranking from silently restoring 3.6-first order while preserving the provider resilience/budget implementation. This counter cannot observe provider calls outside the repository. During the nonpersisting original Writer test, a temporary Gemini client is instantiated with `retry_options.attempts=1`; its client, pool and validation flag are restored in `finally`. These are per-process run guards, not a replacement for the provider's project-wide RPD counter; other users, processes and APIs may share quota.
5. **No false quality signoff:** A valid 5-stage JSON, a copied SHA-256 or a CI GREEN cannot approve the creative article. An actual independently scored editorial comparison remains outstanding. Human editorial and full Fact/Publication assessment are REQUIRED before publishing or enabling persistence.

**CI counterexamples:** `tests/test_v3_p5_routing_boundaries.py` attempts a fourth 3.6 send, an unauthorized `new` creative Writer invocation, a persisted new P5 publication, and a P5 quality retry bypass. All must be rejected. `tests/test_v3_p5_pure_provider.py` tests 3.8-first ordering and that missing nonpersisting authority blocks a new source. These tests are no-API structural checks, not an assertion of article quality.

**Known remaining uncertainty:** the Production prompt's `Phase0Case` evidence shape is built from modern verified source-native metadata rather than the historic frozen Source Summary / What / Why / Decision / Action fixture. Prompt equality on frozen inputs does not imply creative equivalence on a fresh real source. Also the historical 83 was created with an excluded Preview model, and model sampling varies. Do not represent any nonpreview quality as 83 until real blind reading confirms it.

- The canonical `gemini_provider_resilience._deep_dive_generation_config({}, "deep_dive")` injects `thinking_config={"thinking_level": "low"}`. Only while the authenticated original P5 five-stage request is at the final `a_plus_editorial_orchestration._generate_via_chat` send boundary is that single exact injected config stripped to `None`. Any other override, different request context or unverified scope is rejected before sending. The scope flag is always cleared in `finally`.
